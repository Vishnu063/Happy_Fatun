# Local acquisition: owner checklist after core launch

This is a free-first plan, not a lead-volume or sales guarantee. No accounts were created, listings published, domains purchased, paid services configured, or messages sent as part of development.

1. Confirm the working Aaranya Living name and complete Indian trademark/domain checks before committing to a registered identity. Do not imply registration.
2. Publish accurate public phone/email/WhatsApp, actual service areas and any address the owner chooses to disclose in studio Settings. Use the same approved details consistently across channels. Do not invent an address, radius, opening hours, guarantees or reviews.
3. Claim and verify the eligible Google Business Profile through Google's owner verification flow. Select truthful business categories and service-area/address options that match the actual business; do not create an ineligible or virtual location.
4. Replace the clearly labeled demo illustrations through the owner image manager with permitted manufacturer/workshop photos or clearly classified illustrative concepts. Keep studio and room images consistent with the same furniture design.
5. Set up owner-controlled Instagram and WhatsApp Business profiles. Share genuine photos, design explanations and links to descriptive product pages. Request permission before approaching local interior designers or contractors; no automated bulk outreach or unsolicited marketing.
6. Use explicit campaign links such as `?utm_source=google`, `instagram`, `whatsapp` or `referral`. The quote stores only an allowlisted source, not the raw URL or arbitrary query text. Attribution is per browser tab, without a cross-site tracking ID. Source totals and inquiry-to-won ratios are available privately in the Leads tab.
7. Use follow-up notes/dates and stage filters to review consented inquiries. Stage changes do not automatically send messages. Review feasibility with the manufacturer before issuing final prices or confirming orders.
8. After owner-approved launch, choose and verify a privacy-respecting visit analytics setup if needed. Live visit counts are intentionally not fabricated or enabled before launch. Current metrics are recorded inquiries, sources and mapped won inquiries; they are not unique visitor counts and do not prove offline sales. Review consent/privacy requirements for any future analytics service.

Chatwoot/Mautic, paid ads, SMS and bulk outreach remain deferred. Real-world visits, delivery, customer acquisition and external email are unverified until actual deployment and authorized checks take place.
