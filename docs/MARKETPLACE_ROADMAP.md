# Owner-managed local furniture marketplace roadmap

The owner's [business-model decision](https://github.com/Vishnu063/Happy_Fatun/pull/2#issuecomment-6404461966) and [payment update](https://github.com/Vishnu063/Happy_Fatun/pull/2#issuecomment-6404476534) define Aaranya Living as a local marketplace coordinating quotations and supplier fulfillment. Intended initial geography: Kharadi, Hadapsar, Magarpatta, Manjari and Mundhwa. Initial categories remain dining tables, beds, wardrobes, chairs and coffee tables. This intent is not verified stock, supplier participation, guaranteed delivery coverage, a public address or a legal/business compliance finding. Inventory purchases are not assumed.

## Current repository phase

The quote-only public flow remains: browse a labeled concept, request details/quote with required full name and valid Indian mobile, optionally provide email/address/message, explicitly consent to contact, and receive an idempotent receipt. Product identity/name are resolved from the database. Optional customization starts from the shown configuration. Owner Master Control retains private inquiry statuses, contact links, search, notes and follow-up dates, and can open the product's supplier comparison. Shop/supplier sourcing records are private; no supplier relationship is invented. Owner-authorized personal-data removal retains the commercial record while clearing customer contact/address/free-text notes. These are repository/local tests, not live customer transactions.

## Next incremental phase — private order records (NOT IMPLEMENTED)

After reviewing inquiry and supplier essentials, design an additive D1 order record linked to the existing inquiry and the selected product/supplier link. Preserve product/quote snapshots and all legacy statuses. Planned order states: draft, confirmed, ordered, dispatched, delivered, cancelled. Store owner-agreed customer/supplier amounts, delivery costs, actual margin, dates and private notes. Confirm current supplier price/availability before quoting and record customer acceptance before confirming an order. Keep inquiry status distinct from order/payment status. A durable private change audit should identify owner actions and prevent silent conflicting edits. Validate state transitions, non-negative integer-paise amounts, missing/archived supplier references, cancellation, duplicate submissions and failed writes. Unit/API and no-R2 mobile/desktop workflow checks must precede delivery.

## Later phase — manual payment reconciliation (NOT IMPLEMENTED)

The operator, Aaranya Living, will collect payments directly according to the owner's latest decision. Planned flow: inquiry → supplier price/availability confirmation → final owner quote → customer acceptance → owner-verified customer payment → supplier order → dispatch/delivery → reconciliation. Planned private payment states: unpaid, pending verification, partially paid, paid, refunded. Track recorded/verified amounts, a bounded validated transaction reference, supplier payout, delivery expense, refund and actual margin with an auditable owner ledger. Use integer paise, explicit verification dates, safe concurrency/idempotency and consistency checks across partial payments/refunds. A customer-provided reference or screenshot **must never automatically confirm payment**.

Do not collect/store card details, bank credentials, UPI PINs or passwords. Do not add a public checkout, payment gateway or payment instructions now. Real payment acceptance requires verified business payment details and owner-approved refund/cancellation/delivery/privacy policies, explicit activation approval and business/tax compliance review. No bank/UPI details, supplier agreements or legal clearance are invented. Only private manual records are planned; gateways/paid services remain out of scope. Retention and access/audit policies must be reviewed before actual payment metadata is stored.

## Deployment boundary

The current catalog/inquiry/supplier branch is prepared for free Workers + D1 without R2. No order tables, payment records, gateway, checkout or real acceptance were added. No deployment, merge, remote migration, production secret installation, paid-service activation or live database modification is authorized by this roadmap. [The deployment runbook](FREE_PREVIEW_DEPLOYMENT.md) still requires separate explicit owner approval and target/backup review.
